## About

I enjoy working on problems where the answer is not obvious at the start.

My projects tend to sit at the intersection of **systems, data, automation, experimentation, security, and product thinking**. I care less about fitting into a label and more about understanding the problem, choosing the right tools, and producing something that can be tested in the real world.

A few principles I try to keep consistent:

- make complexity legible
- measure before assuming
- automate repetitive work
- design for failure, not just the happy path
- leave systems easier to understand than I found them
